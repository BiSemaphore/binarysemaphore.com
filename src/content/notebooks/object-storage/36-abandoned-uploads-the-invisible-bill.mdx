```text
  300 parts × 32 MiB  =  ~10 GB uploaded into the staging area
        │
        │  the user closes the tab
        ▼
  CompleteMultipartUpload   never called
  AbortMultipartUpload      never called
        │
        ▼
  10 GB sits there. billed every month. forever.
```

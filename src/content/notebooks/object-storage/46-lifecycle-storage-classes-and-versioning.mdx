```text
  day 0    Standard              ~$0.023/GB    hot

  day 90   Glacier Instant       ~$0.004/GB    rare, still milliseconds
  day 180  Glacier Deep Archive  ~$0.00099/GB  compliance, hours to restore
  day 365  expire
```

Objects should not all live forever in the most expensive tier.

```text
  day 0    Standard              ~$0.023/GB    hot
  day 30   Standard-IA           ~$0.0125/GB   occasional, retrieval fee
  day 90   Glacier Instant       ~$0.004/GB    rare, still milliseconds
  day 180  Glacier Deep Archive  ~$0.00099/GB  compliance, hours to restore
  day 365  expire
```

The trap is the minimums: 30 days minimum billable duration on IA, 90 on Glacier
Flexible, 180 on Deep Archive, plus per-object overhead and a transition request
per object. Moving millions of small objects to a cold class <Peach>can cost more
than leaving them in Standard</Peach>. Check the size distribution first.

<DoThis>

Lifecycle rules to write on day one

- <Mint>Abort incomplete multipart uploads after 7 days.</Mint> (<Ref to="#36-abandoned-uploads-the-invisible-bill">Section 36</Ref>. Non-negotiable.)
- <Mint>Delete anything under `pending/` or `tmp/` after 24 hours.</Mint> (<Ref to="#30-reconciliation-pending-expired-orphaned">Section 30</Ref>.)
- <Mint>Expire non-current versions after 30 to 90 days</Mint>, so versioning stays a
  safety net rather than an unbounded cost.
- Only then consider cold-tier transitions.

Three relationships, and they are the whole of relational modelling:

- <Peach>one to one</Peach> `users` to `user_profiles`, implemented by making the foreign
  key the primary key
- <Peach>one to many</Peach> `projects` to `tasks`, implemented by a plain foreign key on
  the many side
- <Peach>many to many</Peach> `users` to `projects`, implemented by a linking table with a
  composite primary key

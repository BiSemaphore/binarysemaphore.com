Numbers stop the design from being hand-waving. Do this arithmetic out loud.

#### Transfer time

```text
10 GB over a 100 Mbit/s uplink

  100 Mbit/s = 12.5 MB/s
  10 GB = 10,240 MB
  10,240 / 12.5 = 819 s = ~13.6 minutes   (best case, single stream)
```

```text
   PROXIED (bad)                        PRESIGNED (good)

  Frontend                              Frontend

     ▼                                     ▼

     │           timeouts, cost            │ 2. returns signed URLs
     │ 10 GB                               ▼
     ▼                                  Frontend
    S3                                     │ 3. 10 GB, direct
```
